# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

We are committed to providing a welcoming and inspiring community for all people, regardless of gender, gender identity and expression, age, sexual orientation, disability, personal appearance, body size, race, ethnicity, religion (or lack thereof), or technology choices.

## Expected Behavior

- **Be respectful**: Treat all community members with dignity and respect.
- **Be inclusive**: Welcome diverse perspectives and lived experiences.
- **Be professional**: Keep discussions focused and constructive.
- **Be supportive**: Help each other grow and learn.

## Unacceptable Behavior

The following behaviors are considered unacceptable:

- Harassment, discrimination, or intimidation of any kind
- Exclusionary language or comments
- Unwelcome advances or requests
- Publishing others' private information without consent
- Other conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate

## Reporting & Enforcement

If you witness or experience unacceptable behavior, please report it to the maintainers. All reports will be reviewed and handled confidentially.

Maintainers who fail to enforce this code of conduct in good faith may face consequences.
